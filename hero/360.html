SPHERICAL VIDEO · EQUIRECTANGULAR 2:1 · MONO

LOOK AROUND.

Drag to look, arrow keys to turn, plus and minus to narrow the view. This viewer renders the equirectangular video through a real spherical projection in raw WebGL (no library). It is not a flat video element.

INTERACTIVE DESIGN CONCEPT · NOT A LIVE DEVICE SESSION

WebGL is unavailable here, so the spherical viewer cannot run. The hero poster on the main page carries the same opening composition.

The video texture is sampled per-pixel by longitude and latitude. If the seam were broken, the view would tear at the wrap point; if the poles were broken, the zenith and nadir would smear or blank.

# HERMES-TV hero: story (story/story.md)

The 20 s loop has no narrator, no audio, and no words in the scene.
Its story is told by motion and by the HTML around it.

## Opening frame (t = 0, also the poster)

Central console, screens glowing cyan. Light paths arc from the hub to
five devices on a ringed platform. Skyline behind, stars above. The
headline above the stage (HTML, not in the scene) states the thesis:
YOUR DEVICES. YOUR AGENTS. YOUR RULES.

## The loop (repeats every 20 s, seamlessly)

- Camera: a slow breath, yaw swinging 0.55 +/- 0.24 rad, pitch bobbing
  gently, distance breathing 13.5 +/- 0.4. The view never cuts, never
  jumps, and returns exactly to the opening frame at t = 20.
- Rings: the platform rings rotate slowly, one clockwise, one counter,
  completing whole turns in 20 s.
- Paths: pulses travel hub to device continuously, two per path,
  staggered, so the network always looks alive but never frantic.
- Task cycle (4 s, five devices in rotation, one per cycle):
  0.0-1.6 s: a white dot travels the light path from hub to device.
  1.6-2.5 s: a receipt card rises above the device with a checkmark.
  2.5-4.0 s: rest. The HTML chip narrates the phase and states, every
  time, that the sequence is illustrative and no live task ran.
- Skyline: windows twinkle on integer-cycle sine waves, so the city
  shimmers without ever breaking the loop.

## The 360 video (same 20 s, same timeline, different camera)

The camera stands above the console at (0, 3.6, 0) and does not move.
The viewer supplies the camera motion by looking around. The console
is below, devices ring the horizon, paths arc overhead to the nadir
point under the camera, and the skyline wraps the full circle. The
seam (where longitude wraps) falls between devices, and the poles are
the sky above and the platform below, never blank.

## What the story refuses to say

The dot is not a real task. The card is not a real receipt. The glow
is not a live connection. Every one of those readings is corrected by
HTML text on the same page, in the same view, before anyone can mistake
the concept for a session.
